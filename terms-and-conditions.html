Description of Service

Wealth-Meter is an on-device personal finance tracking and mathematical calculation utility designed to help users:

  • Record, organize, and track personal multi-asset holdings (e.g., stocks, mutual funds, provident funds, fixed deposits, precious metals, real estate, cash).
  • Compute net worth by balancing gross assets against recorded liabilities and loans.
  • Perform mathematical projections (e.g., SIP, Step-Up SIP, SWP, Loan EMI, Compound Interest, FIRE freedom horizon, and Monte Carlo scenarios).
  • Monitor historical portfolio checkpoints and simulate hypothetical macroeconomic stress tests.
