Portfolio Health & Risk Score (0–100)

Health Index & Runway

An algorithmic 0–100 diagnostic engine grading your portfolio across 5 foundational pillars:

  1. Concentration Risk (Herfindahl-Hirschman Index): Flags single holdings exceeding 25% of total net worth.
  2. Asset Class Diversification: Rewards balanced spread across equities, debt, gold, real estate, and liquid cash.
  3. Emergency Liquid Buffer: Verifies whether liquid reserves cover 3–6 months of living expenses.
  4. High-Risk Volatility Exposure: Monitors overexposure to high-beta assets.
  5. Fixed Income Anchor: Ensures a defensive debt/FD cushion is present to weather market drawdowns.
