Overview & Privacy-First Philosophy

Welcome to Wealth-Meter. Unlike conventional financial applications that require you to link bank accounts, create cloud profiles, or upload your asset ledgers to external servers, Wealth-Meter operates entirely on-device.

We do not run proprietary backend databases that store, process, sell, or monetize your portfolio, balances, transactions, or personal identities.

2

Information We Collect and Process

A. Personal & Financial Portfolio Data (Stored Locally Only)

When you use Wealth-Meter, you may manually enter financial records, including:

  • Asset holdings (stocks, mutual funds, fixed/recurring deposits, provident funds, real estate, precious metals, crypto, and liquid cash).
  • Transaction amounts, purchase prices, quantities, and maturity terms.
  • Liabilities (loans, mortgages, interest APRs, and monthly EMIs).
  • Financial goals, milestone targets, and multi-profile family tags.
🔒 Local Storage Guarantee

All financial data is stored strictly on your local device storage using encrypted Android storage structures (AES-256 GCM). None of your asset quantities, personal names, account values, or financial records are ever transmitted to or stored on our servers.

B. Biometric Authentication Data

If you enable Biometric App Lock (Fingerprint, Face Unlock, or Device PIN):

  • Authentication is handled exclusively by the official Android OS BiometricPrompt and Android KeyStore APIs.
  • Wealth-Meter never accesses, reads, collects, or transmits your biometric samples or raw biometric data. The operating system only returns a cryptographic pass/fail verification token to the app.

C. Live Market Quotes & Currency Conversion Queries

When you refresh current market prices (CMP) or check exchange rates:

  • The app sends outbound network requests to public financial endpoints (e.g., Yahoo Finance API, AMFI mutual fund feeds, Open.er-api FX exchange rate feeds).
  • Only public identifiers (such as ticker symbols like RELIANCE.NS or currency codes like USD/INR) are queried.
  • No private portfolio details, investment amounts, user IDs, or balance figures are ever attached to these price requests.

D. Device and Technical Information

Standard technical diagnostics (such as Android OS version, device model, and network state ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) are evaluated locally by the operating system solely to ensure proper app rendering, internet connectivity detection for live price updates, and responsive layout behavior.

Third-Party Services & SDKs

Wealth-Meter integrates trusted third-party SDKs strictly to provide store billing and free-tier monetization functionalities:

1. Google Play In-App Billing (Google LLC)

  • Used to process one-time purchases for Wealth-Meter Ad-Free Supporter and Investor Pro tiers.
  • Purchases and payment transactions are handled directly by Google Play. We do not collect, process, or store credit card numbers, banking details, or billing addresses.
  • Reference: Google Play Privacy Policy ↗

2. Google Mobile Ads (AdMob by Google LLC)

  • Wealth-Meter displays banner advertisements exclusively to users on the Free tier.
  • AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID AD_ID), general geographic location, and ad interaction metrics to deliver advertisements and prevent ad fraud in accordance with Google's advertising policies.
  • Complete Opt-Out: Users can eliminate all advertisements and third-party advertising tracking permanently by upgrading to the Ad-Free or Pro tier via the in-app membership hub.
  • Reference: Google AdMob Privacy Policy ↗

Data Security & Storage (OWASP MASVS Aligned)

We implement multiple layers of defensive security controls to safeguard your financial privacy on your physical device:

🔐 AES-256 Encryption

Portfolio data and password-protected backups are secured using hardware-backed AES-256 GCM encryption.

🚫 Screen Guard (FLAG_SECURE)

Blocks unauthorized screenshots, screen recordings, and hides portfolio previews in the Android task switcher.

🕶️ Stealth Privacy Mask

One-tap toggle masks exact currency balances with privacy bullets (••••••) when viewing in public spaces.

📡 Zero Account Telemetry

No user databases, no sign-up walls, and no behavioral analytics trackers or marketing pixels.

User Rights, Data Control & Data Deletion

You maintain complete ownership and sovereignty over your financial data at all times:

  • Export Data: Export your complete portfolio ledger at any time in machine-readable JSON, CSV (Excel compatible), or printable Executive PDF statements.
  • Backup & Portability: Backups are saved as plain or AES-256 encrypted payloads directly on your device or personal storage destination of choice.
  • Instant Complete Data Erasure: Permanently delete all local data, cached prices, family profiles, and settings by selecting "Reset All Data" within the in-app Settings dialog, or by clearing application storage via Android System Settings. Once cleared, the data is permanently erased from the device and cannot be recovered by anyone.

Children's Privacy

Wealth-Meter is a financial management tool not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children.

7

International Privacy Compliance

  • GDPR (European Union): Because your personal and financial data remains strictly on your physical hardware, you exercise direct, immediate control to access, rectify, port, and erase your data at any time.
  • CCPA / CPRA (California, USA): We do not sell, rent, or share personal information or financial data to data brokers or third parties for monetary or commercial consideration.
  • DPDP Act (India): Wealth-Meter processes portfolio information strictly on the data principal's device, with zero central cloud storage or cross-border transfer of personal portfolios.
